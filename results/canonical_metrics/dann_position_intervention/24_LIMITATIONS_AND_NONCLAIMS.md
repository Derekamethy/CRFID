# Limitations and nonclaims

- Five training seeds do not support precise population-level mediation.
- DANN suppression is an intervention on learned source representations, not proof of a physical causal mechanism.
- P4 comprises 63 condition blocks; repeated rows are not independent inferential units.
- Lambda selection is source-only and may not optimize target transfer.
- The domain classifier is restricted to source position and does not remove all condition information.
