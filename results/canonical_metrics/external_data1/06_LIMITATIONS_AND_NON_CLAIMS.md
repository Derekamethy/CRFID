# Limitations and non-claims

> Summary of the retained scientific evidence; information-access limitations apply.

- Within-Data1 only; no Paper4 model or class mapping.
- Frozen split is relatively easy; a simple PCA/logistic baseline is nearly perfect.
- Epochs are inherited and not Data1-optimised.
- No physical-frequency-axis equivalence is established.
- Raw CNN exceeds first difference here; no general preprocessing superiority is claimed.
- Results do not establish deployment generalisation.
