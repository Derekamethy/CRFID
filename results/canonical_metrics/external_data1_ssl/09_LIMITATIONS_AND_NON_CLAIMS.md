# Limitations and non-claims

> Summary of the retained scientific evidence; information-access limitations apply.

- No reliable Data1-assisted benefit; no SSL treatment beats T0.
- H1 improves only 2/5 seeds and is below 0.02 and H5 implementation difference.
- Seed variation dominates treatment effects.
- Source-side ranking does not predict P4 ranking (Spearman -0.20).
- Performance is weak/near chance and class collapse is common; “at or below chance” is
  not literally true for every aggregate accuracy.
- Physical-frequency alignment is unresolved; ordered-position crops are not a shared
  physical grid.
- Programme-level historical P4 awareness prevents calling the wider programme fully
  prospective, even though this branch's official evaluation was sealed.
- Evaluation-time `stages.py` bytes were not preserved, only its verified digest.
